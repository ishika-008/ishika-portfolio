# Product

## Platform

Web. Plain HTML and CSS, deployed as static files on GitHub Pages from `/docs`.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and professional contacts who arrive from a link (resume, LinkedIn, message), skim fast, and want to know who Ishika is, what she does now, what she has built, and how to reach her.

## Purpose

A professional portfolio for Ishika Chowdhury: HR at Koundinyasa Technologies Pvt Ltd, with an MCA in Data Science and a BCA. Success: a visitor understands her role, education and projects in under a minute and contacts her.

## Content rules

- Include only: current work and company, the Data Analytics internship (LLMNEXTGEN2AI Pvt. Ltd., January to April 2025), graduation (BCA), master's (MCA in Data Science), and two projects (Finger Detection System, ETL Data Pipeline for Sales Analysis).
- Include no school-level education. The location is shown as just "Bangalore", with no "Hometown" label.
- No phone number anywhere on the site. Public contact is email only; LinkedIn URL not yet provided.
- Never invent dates, results, links or metrics. The MCA institution and years, and the HR start date, have not been provided and are left out.
- Anything else from the old resume (courses, interests) is left out unless the owner asks for it.
